About PerformPredict

PerformPredict puts two pre-trained machine learning models behind simple forms. Enter a student's details and get an estimate: a mathematics score for school students, or a grade category for college students.

Nothing is retrained and nothing is stored. Every prediction runs on the saved models, and the dashboard reports their evaluation results as they are, including the weak ones.

Platform features

Two prediction tools

Separate forms and models for school and college students.
